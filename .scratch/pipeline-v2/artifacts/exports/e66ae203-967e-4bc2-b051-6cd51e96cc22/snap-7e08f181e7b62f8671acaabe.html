Fruits Vocabulary

Common Fruits

Common Fruits
Sweet, juicy, and packed with vitamins. Practice saying each word aloud.
apple A round fruit with red, green, or yellow skin and crisp white flesh. I eat an apple every morning for a healthy snack.
banana A long curved fruit with a thick yellow peel and soft sweet flesh. She sliced a banana into her cereal bowl.
grapes Small round fruits that grow in bunches, green or purple. We bought a bunch of grapes for the picnic.
orange A round citrus fruit with a tough orange rind and juicy segments. He peeled the orange and shared the segments with his friend.
strawberry A small red fruit with tiny seeds on the outside, sweet and fragrant. Strawberry ice cream is my favorite flavor.
watermelon A large oval fruit with a green rind, red juicy flesh, and black seeds. Nothing beats cold watermelon on a hot summer day.

Which of these fruits grow on trees? Which grow on vines or bushes?

Remember: fruits usually have seeds inside or on the outside and are often sweet.

Vegetables Vocabulary

Common Vegetables

Common Vegetables
Crunchy, colorful, and full of nutrients. Practice saying each word aloud.
carrot A long orange root vegetable that is crunchy and sweet. She grated carrots into her salad.
broccoli A green vegetable with a tree-like head full of florets. He likes broccoli steamed with a little cheese.
bell pepper A sweet pepper that comes in red, yellow, or green, shaped like a bell. She sliced red bell pepper for the stir-fry.
spinach Dark green leafy leaves that are soft and full of iron. Add fresh spinach to your omelet for extra nutrition.
tomato A juicy red fruit often used as a vegetable in salads and sauces. She sliced tomatoes for her sandwich.
cucumber A long green vegetable with a mild taste and lots of water. He likes cucumber slices with hummus as a snack.

Which of these vegetables are leafy? Which are root vegetables?

Try to eat a rainbow of vegetables each day for the best nutrition.
