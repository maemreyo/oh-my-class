Remember
Stative verbs (know, believe, belong, etc.) are not used in continuous forms.
Erroneous SentenceCorrected SentenceExplanation
I am knowing him for two years.I have known him for two years.Know is a stative verb; use present perfect with for/since.
She go to school every day.She goes to school every day.Third‑person singular needs -s in present simple.
I have lived here since 5 years.I have lived here for five years.Since points to a specific start; for is used with a length of time.
We are having breakfast at 8 a.m. every morning.We have breakfast at 8 a.m. every morning.Habitual actions use present simple, not continuous.
They have been waiting for the bus since 20 minutes.They have been waiting for the bus for 20 minutes.For + length of time with present perfect continuous.
