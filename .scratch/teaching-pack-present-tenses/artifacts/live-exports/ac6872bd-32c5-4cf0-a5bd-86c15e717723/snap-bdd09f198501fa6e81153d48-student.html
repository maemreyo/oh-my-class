Present Tenses Mastery Quiz – Inverse Thinking for Vietnamese Learners

teaching Quiz Overview & Common Error Patterns Four Tenses, Three Frequent Pitfalls This quiz tests your ability to spot and correct the three most common errors Vietnamese learners make with present tenses: (1) using stative verbs like know, want, love in any continuous form, (2) forgetting the third-person singular -s in present simple, and (3) confusing present perfect simple with present perfect continuous, especially with since/for and result vs. duration. Quick reference Present Simple = routine or permanent truth. Present Continuous = action happening now or a temporary situation around now. Present Perfect Simple = past action with a present result. Present Perfect Continuous = past action continuing to now, emphasis on duration.
