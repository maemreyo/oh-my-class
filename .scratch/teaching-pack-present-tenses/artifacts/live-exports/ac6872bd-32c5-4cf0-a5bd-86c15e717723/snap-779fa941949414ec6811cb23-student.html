teaching Listen & Spot Errors Listen & Spot Errors Play a 60‑second audio clip of Vietnamese learners making typical present‑tense mistakes (e.g., 'I am knowing him for two years', 'She go to school every day'). Listen carefully and note which verb forms sound wrong. Focus on the time markers (for, since, every day) and whether the verb describes a state or an action.
teaching Learning Objectives Learning Objectives By the end of this lesson you will be able to: 1. Identify common Vietnamese‑speaker errors in the four present tenses. 2. Explain why each erroneous sentence violates grammar rules, referencing time markers and verb form constraints. 3. Correct erroneous sentences into the appropriate present tense form. 4. Analyze a short mixed‑tense paragraph and justify tense choices for each verb. 5. Create an original mini‑dialogue that correctly deploys all four present tenses in meaningful context.
