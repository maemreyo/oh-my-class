Discovering Animals: Names and Features

Type: lesson | Theme: default

Welcome to our animal adventure! Today we will learn about five exciting animals: lion, elephant, dolphin, eagle, and kangaroo. We will discover their names, look at pictures, and talk about what makes each animal special.

• Lion – a big cat with a mane that lives in groups called prides. • Elephant – the largest land animal with a long trunk. • Dolphin – a friendly sea mammal that uses sound to find food. • Eagle – a bird that can see far and has a sharp hooked beak. • Kangaroo – a hopping marsupial that carries babies in a pouch.

1. Look at the picture of each animal and shout its name. 2. Describe one feature of each animal in a sentence. 3. In pairs, ask each other: "What does the elephant use its trunk for?"

Great job! Remember the five animals and one special thing about each. We will review them again next week with a fun quiz.
