1. Executive Summary

The Gap: oh-my-class has a mature 6-layer content quality gate system but zero infrastructure for measuring learning effectiveness. The system validates that teaching packs are well-structured HTML with correct answers — but cannot tell you whether they actually teach.

The research reveals three converging streams of evidence for closing this loop:

Learning Science

Kirkpatrick L2, Bloom's Mastery, and the RISE Framework converge: effectiveness requires pre/post student outcome data. RISE found that continuous improvement cycles matter more than raw usage (65% of growth across 4 semesters).

Knowledge Tracing

BKT provides cold-start-friendly foundation; KT4EQG demonstrates the full closed loop: concept selector → LLM generator → contrastive verifier → student attempt → KT update. Maps directly to oh-my-class's Content Creator + Reviewer.

Closed-Loop Systems

Plot-Ark, Khan Academy, and EduRL-GPT all demonstrate the full cycle. Khan Academy's +6.1% improvement came from cumulative measured iterations, not single transformative changes. The architecture is ready — what's missing is engineering.

3.3 The RISE Framework — Key Insight

"More visits to an OER do not improve achievement, but continuous improvement cycles of targeted OER do."

The RISE classification accounted for 65% of growth in student performance across 4 semesters (190 learning objectives, HLM/GCM methodology). Mere exposure is statistically inert. What matters is whether practitioners acted on the analytic signal.

4. Knowledge Tracing as the Mathematical Bridge

4.1 BKT — The Cold-Start Foundation

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing models student knowledge as a Hidden Markov Model with 4 parameters per knowledge component:

  • P(L₀) — prior probability of mastery
  • P(L) — learn rate (transition from not-learned to learned)
  • P(G) — guess rate (correct when not-learned)
  • P(S) — slip rate (incorrect when learned)

pyBKT library (Carnegie Mellon, 249 stars, MIT): trainable in <1 hour on a laptop, fits a dozen students. This is the pragmatic starting point for oh-my-class.

4.2 KT4EQG — The Full Closed Loop

KT4EQG (UCSB, arXiv 2605.23933) demonstrates the exact pattern oh-my-class needs:

Student History → KT2 (EM on KC tree) → Concept Selector (argmax mastery gain) → LLM Generator (Qwen3-8B SFT+GRPO) → Contrastive Verifier (hard negatives) → Student Attempt → KT Update → Next Cycle

Key design choices:

  • 2D selection: picks both concept AND difficulty (not just one)
  • Contrastive verifier trained on sibling-KC hard negatives — catches concept drift
  • Cold start: BKT/DKT as surrogate, initial_r_diff seeding from config
