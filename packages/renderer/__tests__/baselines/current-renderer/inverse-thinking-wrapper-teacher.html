The Vanishing Time Marker (Case A)

Hiện trường A student writes: I have visited Da Nang yesterday.
  • yesterday marks a finished time
  • present perfect avoids finished-time markers
Vùng an toàn

✓ Use simple past with finished time: I visited Da Nang yesterday.

Ghi chú hồ sơ: Finished time markers push the verb phrase into simple past.

Nhiệm vụ học sinh
→ Identify the clue that makes the sentence unsafe and rewrite it.
Đáp án & lý giải (giáo viên)

Đáp án: I visited Da Nang yesterday.

Lý giải: The adverb yesterday conflicts with present perfect usage.
