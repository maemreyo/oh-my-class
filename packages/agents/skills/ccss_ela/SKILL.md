# Curriculum Skill: Common Core State Standards — English Language Arts

When creating ELA/English content, align to CCSS ELA standards.

## Anchor Standards (apply across K-12)

### Reading
- RL/RI.1 — Cite textual evidence
- RL/RI.2 — Determine central idea or theme
- RL/RI.3 — Analyze how individuals, events, ideas develop

### Writing
- W.1 — Write arguments with evidence
- W.2 — Informative/explanatory texts
- W.3 — Narrative writing

### Language
- L.1 — Command of grammar and usage
- L.2 — Command of conventions (capitalization, punctuation)
- L.4 — Vocabulary acquisition and use

## Grade Band Focus

### K-2
- Phonics and word recognition (RF.3)
- Reading foundational skills — fluency (RF.4)
- Print concepts and phonological awareness

### 3-5
- Literature and informational text comprehension
- Research and inquiry projects
- Opinion and informative writing

### 6-8
- Analyzing arguments and rhetoric in text
- Cite strong textual evidence
- Write arguments and explanatory essays

### 9-12
- Close reading of complex literary and informational texts
- Evaluate multiple sources for evidence
- Research-based argument essays

## How to Apply

Specify the standard code in objectives:

> [RL.5.2] Students will determine the theme and summarize the text.
