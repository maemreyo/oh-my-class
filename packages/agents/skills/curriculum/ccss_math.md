# Curriculum Skill: Common Core State Standards — Mathematics

When creating math content, align learning objectives and activities to CCSS Math standards.

## Grade Band Standards

### K-2
- Count, add, subtract within 20 (K.CC, 1.OA, 2.OA)
- Understand place value (1.NBT, 2.NBT)
- Measure lengths, tell time (1.MD, 2.MD)

### 3-5
- Multiply and divide within 100 (3.OA)
- Operations with whole numbers and fractions (3.NF, 4.NF, 5.NF)
- Geometric measurement and area (3.MD, 4.MD, 5.MD)

### 6-8
- Ratios and proportional relationships (6.RP, 7.RP)
- The Number System — rational and irrational numbers (6.NS, 7.NS, 8.NS)
- Expressions and equations (6.EE, 7.EE, 8.EE)
- Linear equations and functions (8.F)

### 9-12
- Number and Quantity (N-RN, N-CN, N-VM)
- Algebra — seeing structure in expressions (A-SSE, A-APR, A-CED, A-REI)
- Functions — building and interpreting (F-IF, F-BF, F-LE, F-TF)
- Statistics and Probability (S-ID, S-IC, S-CP, S-MD)

## How to Apply

Prefix each learning objective with the relevant standard code:

> [3.OA.A.1] Students will interpret products of whole numbers.

Include the standard code in the `curriculum_standard` field of the lesson plan.
