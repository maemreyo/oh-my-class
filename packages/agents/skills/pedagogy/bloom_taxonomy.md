# Pedagogy Skill: Bloom's Revised Taxonomy

Use Bloom's Revised Taxonomy when writing learning objectives. Each objective should
specify a measurable verb at the appropriate cognitive level.

## The Six Levels (low → high)

### 1. Remember
Recall facts and basic concepts.
**Verbs**: define, list, recall, recognize, identify, name, state

### 2. Understand
Explain ideas or concepts.
**Verbs**: describe, explain, summarize, classify, compare, interpret, paraphrase

### 3. Apply
Use information in new situations.
**Verbs**: use, solve, demonstrate, calculate, execute, implement, operate

### 4. Analyze
Draw connections among ideas.
**Verbs**: differentiate, organize, attribute, examine, distinguish, break down

### 5. Evaluate
Justify a decision or course of action.
**Verbs**: argue, defend, judge, critique, assess, appraise, prioritize

### 6. Create
Produce new or original work.
**Verbs**: design, assemble, construct, develop, formulate, author, generate

## How to Apply

Every learning objective should follow this pattern:

> Students will be able to **[verb at appropriate Bloom level]** [content/object].

Example:
> Students will be able to **compare** (Understand) the life cycles of mammals and insects.

Lesson plans for higher-grade content should include at least one objective at Analyze or above.
